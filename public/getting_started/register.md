# Get the Activation Key

SafeSquid activation depends on a key issued through the Self-Service Portal. Without the key, operators cannot complete the deployment checkpoint before policy enforcement. Treat the key as deployment evidence and store it through approved operational procedures.

## Validate prerequisites

Before registering, confirm:

- The deployment owner has an approved email address.
- The organization knows which team owns the Self-Service Portal account.
- The activation key will be stored in an approved secure location.
- The SafeSquid instance will have required outbound access for activation.

## Register in the portal

1. Open the [SafeSquid Self-Service Portal](https://key.safesquid.com).
2. Create or sign in to the deployment account.
3. Complete the required account details.
4. Generate or download the activation key.
5. Store the key according to the organization's secret-handling process.

Do not paste activation keys into tickets, chat, screenshots, or public documentation.

## Verify registration evidence

Capture:

- Portal account owner.
- Date the key was generated.
- Target deployment or instance name.
- Secure storage location reference.
- Operator responsible for activation.

## Troubleshoot registration

| Symptom | Likely cause | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| Portal email not received | Mail filtering or wrong address | Check mail quarantine and confirm address |
| Key not visible | Account state or entitlement issue | Confirm portal account and subscription status |
| Key cannot be stored securely | Process gap | Pause activation until secure storage is available |
| Activation later fails | Wrong key or outbound reachability issue | Confirm key source and network access during activation |
