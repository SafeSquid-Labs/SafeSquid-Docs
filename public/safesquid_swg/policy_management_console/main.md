# Policy Management Console

The Policy Management Console is the administrator workbench for SafeSquid SWG. Use it to review reports, configure controls, and reach support workflows without separating policy intent from operational evidence.

## Quickstart path

1. [Reports](/safesquid_swg/policy_management_console/reports) — review access, security, and operational evidence before and after policy changes.
2. [Configure](/safesquid_swg/policy_management_console/configure) — manage web security, authentication, SSL inspection, DLP, and related settings.
3. [Support](/safesquid_swg/policy_management_console/support) — collect support evidence and align troubleshooting with the active configuration.
