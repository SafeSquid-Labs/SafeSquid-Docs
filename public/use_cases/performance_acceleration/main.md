# Speed Up Browsing With Performance Acceleration

Faster pages and controlled bandwidth both reduce load on the link.

## Quickstart path

1. **[Performance Accelerators](/use_cases/performance_acceleration/performance_accelerators)** - use SafeSquid performance accelerators such as caching, prefetching, bandwidth management, speed limits, and WCCP with deployment-aware expectations.
2. **[Caching and Prefetching](/use_cases/performance_acceleration/caching)** - configure SafeSquid content caching and prefetching to cut bandwidth and latency, and verify objects are actually being served from the local store.
3. **[Bandwidth Management](/use_cases/performance_acceleration/manage_bandwidth)** - control download speed, max download size, and upload size per user with SafeSquid Limits for bandwidth and data-handling policies.
4. **[Performance Plot](/use_cases/performance_acceleration/performance_plot)** - generate SafeSquid performance plots to investigate load, capacity, and timing issues with evidence that supports troubleshooting and operations review.
5. **[Speed Limits](/use_cases/performance_acceleration/speed_limits)** - configure connection speed limits in SafeSquid to control per-user or per-group bandwidth consumption.
