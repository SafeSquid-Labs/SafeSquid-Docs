# Map Evidence, Not Marketing Claims

This page helps security and assurance teams identify where a SafeSquid control may contribute evidence. It is not legal advice, a statement of applicability, an audit conclusion, or certification. Verify the current authoritative requirement, implementation scope, operating effectiveness, and evidence period with the responsible legal, risk, and audit teams.

## Control contribution matrix

| Assurance objective | SafeSquid contribution to evaluate | Evidence to collect | External dependencies and limitations |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Govern web access | Identity-aware policy, profiling, destination and application controls | Approved policy, owner, positive and negative tests, change history, decision logs | Identity quality, endpoint steering, bypass governance, direct egress controls |
| Protect encrypted web traffic | Authorized TLS inspection, certificate validation, managed bypass | Inspection scope, CA fingerprint, trust rollout, inspect and bypass tests, certificate events | PKI, endpoint trust, application pinning, privacy and legal restrictions |
| Prevent malicious content | Malware scanners, threat intelligence, DNS and content controls | Update state, benign test result, action, alert, event, failure test | Feed and scanner availability, encrypted or unsupported traffic, tuning |
| Reduce data leakage | Upload, content, MIME, text, image, and destination controls | Synthetic positive and negative tests, matched policy, action, event, exception | Data classification, approved destinations, encryption, archives, unsupported formats |
| Attribute activity | User, device, network, time, request, and action context | Authentication result, group mapping, synchronized event, exception record | Directory, time, endpoint identity, shared devices, service accounts |
| Preserve audit evidence | Transaction logs, security events, reports, and forwarding | Field completeness, timestamp accuracy, forwarding receipt, retention and access review | Storage, SIEM, integrity protection, capacity, privacy, review process |
| Detect and respond | Alerts, logs, diagnostics, support bundle, policy and service state | Incident timeline, correlation data, affected scope, correction and recovery tests | SOC process, monitoring coverage, escalation, evidence handling |
| Maintain resilience | Clustering, backups, restoration, configuration sync, monitored dependencies | Health and failover tests, backup result, restore test, RTO and RPO result | Load balancing, capacity, storage, network, runbooks, release compatibility |
| Manage change | Configuration archive, test matrix, approvals, rollback | Before and after state, reviewer, successful tests, rollback result | Change process, segregation of duties, source control, privileged access |
| Protect personal data | Access, inspection scope, DLP, logging minimization, retention controls | Purpose and scope decision, data-flow record, access review, retention and deletion evidence | Lawful basis, notices, data-principal rights, processor contracts, legal interpretation |

## Mapping method

<Steps>
  <Step title="Confirm applicability">
    The legal, risk, or audit owner identifies the current requirement and affected systems. Do not begin with a product feature.
  </Step>
  <Step title="State the control objective">
    Write an outcome that can be tested, including scope and expected failure behavior.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Identify the complete mechanism">
    Include SafeSquid policy, endpoint steering, identity, PKI, dependencies, logging, monitoring, and human process.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Run positive, negative, and failure tests">
    Prove both the intended decision and the boundary where the control does not apply.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Protect and review evidence">
    Confirm time, completeness, integrity, access, retention, and independent review.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Record residual gaps">
    Document exceptions, unsupported traffic, unverified behavior, temporary measures, owner, expiry, and risk acceptance.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Wording for assurance records

Use: **SafeSquid contributes to this control objective when the documented configuration, dependencies, scope, tests, and evidence are operating as approved.**

Avoid: **Enabling SafeSquid makes the organization compliant.**
