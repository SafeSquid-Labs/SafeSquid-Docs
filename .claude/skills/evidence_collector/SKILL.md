# Evidence Collector

Use this skill when a page needs proof-oriented validation or troubleshooting content.

## Goal

Reduce ambiguity by collecting and describing evidence that a reader can reproduce.

## Preferred evidence

- UI confirmation states
- Log entries or log field patterns
- Report names and export locations
- Positive test outcomes
- Negative test outcomes
- Traffic behavior or policy outcomes
- Screenshots when text alone is insufficient

## Workflow

1. Identify what the reader must prove.
2. Identify what evidence is available in SafeSquid or the surrounding environment.
3. State where the evidence appears.
4. State what success looks like.
5. State what misleading success looks like, if relevant.
6. Feed the results into validation and troubleshooting sections.

## Rules

- Prefer evidence that is observable in production-like conditions.
- Do not add screenshots that repeat obvious text.
- Do not claim evidence exists unless you can point to it precisely.
- Include both proof of success and proof of failure when useful.
