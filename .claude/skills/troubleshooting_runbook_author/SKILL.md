# Troubleshooting Runbook Author

Use this skill when documentation must help operators isolate and fix failures under pressure.

## Goal

Produce symptom-first troubleshooting that reduces support dependency and speeds safe recovery.

## Required format

For each issue, cover:

1. Symptom
2. Likely causes
3. Isolation steps
4. Remediation
5. Retest / expected outcome
6. Escalation boundary if unresolved

## What good looks like

- Failure patterns are realistic.
- Checks are specific and observable.
- Remediation is exact, not generic.
- Retest proves whether the fix actually worked.

## Rules

- Do not say only "check logs."
- Do not list causes without a way to distinguish them.
- Do not end with "contact support" unless the page explains what evidence to gather first.
